SnapSort Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

SnapSort is an offline-first organizer for screenshots and receipts. SnapSort does not create user accounts, display advertising, or upload your imported images and item details to SnapSort servers.

Data the app handles

The app processes images you explicitly choose, along with titles, notes, categories, tags, merchant names, amounts, currencies, and purchase dates you enter. This information is stored in the app's private storage on your device.

Reading text on images

SnapSort can read the text on images you add, to fill in the merchant, amount, and date. This runs entirely on your device; images and text are not sent anywhere. You can turn it off in Settings.

Payment notifications (optional)

If you turn on payment capture and grant Android's Notification access, SnapSort reads notifications from payment and banking apps such as GCash, Maya, and banks. It keeps only notifications that look like payments, and skips messaging apps and one-time codes. It stores the amount, counterparty, reference number, and the notification text in the app's private storage to create receipts. Nothing leaves your device. You can turn capture off in Settings or revoke Notification access in Android settings at any time; turning capture off discards notifications that were not yet filed.

Screenshot inbox and shared images (optional)

If you use the screenshot inbox and grant photo access, SnapSort looks at your recent screenshots on the device to find payment confirmations and offers them for you to add. Images you share into SnapSort from other apps are copied into app storage. Screenshots are read on the device only and are not uploaded.

Sharing

SnapSort does not sell or share your library with third parties. When you choose Share, Android sends the selected image to the app or service you select. That destination's privacy practices then apply.

Retention and deletion

Items remain on the device until you delete them, erase app data, or uninstall the app. Deleting a SnapSort item removes the app-managed copy after the undo period and does not delete the source image from your gallery.

Diagnostics

SnapSort does not include a third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Google Play may provide aggregated stability information under Google's own terms when the app is distributed through Google Play.

Security

Imported images are stored in Android app-private storage. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, so users should maintain an exported backup when that feature becomes available.

Changes and contact

This policy must be updated before the app's data practices change. Before public release, replace this paragraph with the developer's legal name and a monitored privacy support email, and publish this page at a stable public HTTPS address.
